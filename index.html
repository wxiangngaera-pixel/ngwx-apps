Questions

What's the difference between subscription and one-time?

A subscription covers hosting, updates and support for as long as you pay; cancel anytime from your account. A one-time purchase is a lifetime licence for that app, hosted for you under fair use, with 12 months of updates included.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. You point a domain or subdomain to the app and I handle the SSL certificate. If you don't have one yet, your app runs on a subdomain I provide.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cards, FPX online banking and GrabPay through Stripe. You receive a Stripe receipt for every payment.

Can the app be customised?

Branding, content and settings are part of setup. Larger changes are quoted separately — just WhatsApp me.

How long until my app is live?

Usually 2–5 working days after you complete the setup form.
